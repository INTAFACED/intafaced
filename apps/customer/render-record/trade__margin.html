Margin ratio is maintenance margin divided by margin plus unrealised PnL. The Healthy, Watch and Danger words are our reading of it (50%, 80%, 100%), not the platform’s liquidation trigger. A position shows — until the service publishes the inputs.

Margin rules

Margin mode
Isolated
Cross margin
Not offered. A shared pool across positions would let one loss reach another.
Portfolio margin
Not offered. Its offset and floor rules are not published.
Hedge mode
No position-mode setting is published. Until the platform sets one, new positions are refused.
Collateral class
Cash only. Yield-bearing, staked and lending balances are refused as margin.

Switch margin mode

Margin mode is chosen when a position opens. The service does not switch it later, so this control stays off.

Spot margin and borrowing

Spot margin is not offered

Borrowing to buy spot has no borrow or repay service, so spot buys are paid in full from your balance. Nothing is borrowed on your behalf.

How liquidation works

  • The platform watches each position’s mark price. When a position can no longer cover its maintenance margin it is closed in steps, not all at once where that is safe.
  • While a liquidation step is running, changes to that position are refused.
  • A loss that has been posted is not reversed. A later correction is a new entry.
  • If a position cannot be closed at a fair price, the platform’s last-resort steps are published in the ADL notice on the futures desk. Read the ADL notice
