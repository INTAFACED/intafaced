# Founder console design

Nitro approved the Raycast-inspired dark/lime direction on 2026-10-10. App-local tokens and component decisions live in design.json.

Use self-hosted Inter, the existing INTAFACED aperture/wordmark, graphite surfaces and deliberate lime actions. Keep rows easy to scan, forms legible and IDs internal. Native controls provide keyboard behavior; phone actions have 44px targets. Control feedback lasts 180ms and is disabled for reduced motion. Trading-direction and danger colours retain their meanings.

The public welcome owns the sculptural introduction; the private console stays quiet for daily review, follow-up and attributed account controls. Completion requires desktop/mobile screenshots, loaded-font inspection and an interaction recording.
