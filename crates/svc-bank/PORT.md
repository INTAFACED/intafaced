# svc-bank in Rust: the port notes

Each slice of svc-bank's move to Rust starts here, in a fresh thread. `services/svc-bank` stays the service that runs until the switch; nothing is served from this crate in any stack yet. This crate holds the shell every slice builds on:

- `src/door/`: every call `router.ts` serves (530: 508 scoped, one `anyScopeProcedure`, 11 job calls, five service-only, five public), who may call each, and its input check (`schemas.json`, run by the kit's zod engine with `src/refines.rs`); and the three REST doors `index.ts` mounts through an exported register function (`src/door/rest.rs`: `POST /internal/closure-check`, `/internal/agents/team-membership`, `/internal/agents/usage-payment`) as far as Fastify's body parser, the signed service and the body's own check. Past those a call answers "svc-bank in Rust: the `<slice>` slice is not ported" (500). `health` is the door's own and is served.
- `src/env.rs`: the environment, held to `env.ts` by `tests/env_parity.rs` (37 environments).
- `src/errors.rs`: `BankError` (every code of `BANK_ERROR_STATUS`, with its tRPC code, its customer copy and whether the code follows the sentence, listed in `src/errors_table.rs`: 612 codes) and `toTrpcError`, the main router's guard.
- The store (slice `store`): `src/db.rs` (one pool, `search_path` `bank,public` and the service's `application_name`, the transaction helper, the boot's schema check; the recovery lock's own one-connection pool, `<SERVICE_NAME>-recovery`; `DATABASE_SSL=true` refuses to start, as the kit's pool has no TLS), `src/migrate.rs` and `migrations/` (the 127 migrations of `services/svc-bank/drizzle`, byte for byte, run by the kit's runner: `svc-bank migrate [--down]`, a reversal only for the files the record names), `src/ready.rs` (`/ready`), `src/door/jobs.rs` (the 27 job doors, `requireService`, `sendBankJobRefuse`, and svc-ops' two reads), `src/restore_watermark.rs`, `src/recovery.rs` (the recovery jobs' lock, pass and back-off), `src/timers.rs` (the other timers and their loop) and `src/main.rs` (the binary, in `index.ts`'s boot order). The door holds the store and passes it to every slice.

`scripts/record-door.mjs` writes the recorded files from the TypeScript: it runs each call's middlewares on probe callers to read its checks, records zod's own verdict on inputs made from each schema, the environments of `scripts/env-cases.json`, and the error table; the slices are `scripts/slices.json`. Its `--check` fails when a written file is stale. Run it with `TZ=UTC` and the workspace packages built (`pnpm --filter "@intafaced/svc-bank^..." build`).

## What the shell holds, and how it is proved

- `tests/wire_replay.rs` sends every request of `services/svc-bank/src/wire-replay.golden/` (530 calls and the REST doors, five callers: 38,376 answers) to the door. Every answer the door decides is held byte for byte (36,268: route and method refusals, 401s, scope, region and service 403s, input 400s, and `health`). Any other answer is owed by its call's slice while the door answers "not ported", counted per slice (2,108). `WIRE_REPLAY_SLICE=<slice>` prints a slice's owed answers; `WIRE_REPLAY_STRICT=<slice>` fails while it owes any. A slice is done when its strict run passes.
- `tests/guard_grid.rs`: each guarded call's middlewares before its input, run by the recorder on 198 callers (no one, or a person with no scope or every scope, at each tier, with and without a second factor, on a session, a key or a sub-account, in a served and an unserved region, with and without a signing service): 103,950 answers, each the door's first refusal word for word or its pass. The wire replay's callers are all fully verified; this is where a call's tier and its module's KYC rule are held.
- `tests/input_parity.rs`: 18,255 inputs over the 530 calls and the two REST doors with a contract schema, each against zod's own verdict (`tests/fixtures/input-parity/<slice>.golden.json`).
- `tests/closure_body.rs`: `parseClosureCheckBody`'s verdicts, held to the TypeScript's.
- `tests/env_parity.rs`: the environment, value for value and refusal for refusal, and for each environment that loads, the `/ready` body `index.ts` builds from it (recorded by `scripts/env-probe.mjs`).
- `tests/store_pg.rs`, on a real Postgres (`TEST_DATABASE_URL`): the binary's `migrate` up, re-run and `--down` (a file never applied left alone), the 175 tables, the schema check, the pool's session settings, the watermark door (svc-ops alone; the counts, digest and stamp), a job door's 401 and its slice, the recovery lock between two replicas, `/health` and `/ready`.

## The doors

The checks run in the TypeScript's middleware order (`src/door/mod.rs`):

1. route and method (`GET` for a query, `POST` for a mutation; no batching);
2. who may call:
   - a scoped call: a signed-in principal (401), then `scopedProcedure`'s guards: the scope (`bank:read`, `bank:write`, `admin:*`, `support:ops` or `trade:withdraw`, with the kit's interactive-only and two-factor rules), then the region: `module: 'bank'` with its KYC rule (tier `full` in a served region, 429 calls); `convertCash.carry` instead asks tier `full` itself, then the region alone;
   - `ops.poolTotals`: any of `admin:read`, `admin:treasury` (`anyScopeProcedure`);
   - the 11 job calls (`ops.runDueTransfers`, `ops.accrueInterest`, `bills.runDue`, ...): a verified service that is svc-bank itself (`requireBankJobService`);
   - the five service-only calls: a verified service their contract names (`serviceProcedureFor`);
   - the five public calls are checked by nobody at the door: `health`, `autoInvest.policy`, `business.policy`, `eligibility.get`, `safety.regionNotice`;
3. the input;
4. the slice.

A REST door reads its body as Fastify does (JSON or text, the prototype-poison guard, 415 and 400 with Fastify's words), then a signed service with the body bound (401), then the one service it is for (403), then the body (400), each with the door's own code.

## What the calls share

- **The router** (`src/router.ts`, `createBankRouter`): mounts every sub-router. The ones built from the shared context (`src/router/context.ts`, `createRouterContext`) live in `src/router/*.router.ts`; the rest are made in their feature folder (`src/<area>/<area>.router.ts`) and handed `bank` and `guard`. `bank` is `BankServices` (`src/bank-service.ts`), assembled in `src/index.ts` from each folder's `compose.ts`.
- **The guard** (`src/router/errors.ts`: `guard`, `bankRefusal`, `toTrpcError`): every handler runs inside `guard`. It maps `BankError` (`src/errors.ts`; codes and statuses in `BANK_ERROR_STATUS`, `src/bank-error-table.ts`), `LedgerError` and `InsufficientFundsError` from the ledger client, `DualControlError` (`src/dual-control.ts`), `ActionApprovalConsumeError` and `LedgerFrozenRefusal`. Nothing is passed through by default; user-facing words come from `src/user-copy.ts`. Each folder's `bank-errors.ts` holds its own codes. Calls mounted from a record (`mountRecord`) use `bankRefusal`.
- **Who may call**: `scopedProcedure('bank:read' | 'bank:write', { module: 'bank' })` for customer calls, `admin:read` / `admin:treasury` for the console's `ops.*`. The 11 job calls (`ops.runDueTransfers`, `ops.accrueInterest`, `bills.runDue`, ...) are `jobProcedure` (`src/router.ts`): HMAC signed as svc-bank, `requireBankJobService` (`src/ops-job-hmac.ts`). The five service-only calls take a verified service named in their contract (`packages/contracts/src/bank-*.ts`).
- **Step-up on money out** (`src/step-up/step-up-gate.ts`, `step-up-store.ts`, `money-out-fields.ts`; table `step_up_uses`): a send to another person, a business payment, card unfreeze or issue needs a fresh approval consumed at svc-identity. `BANK_STEP_UP_MONEY_OUT=off` (the default) makes it a no-op; `required` with no `IDENTITY_URL` refuses `bank.step_up_unavailable`. Ported with `transfers`, the first slice that needs it.
- **Ops approvals** (`src/action-approval-consume.ts`): every `ops.*` mutation that changes an owner value consumes an identity-issued, action-bound approval (`consumeOpsApproval`, `consumeOpsApprovalPair` for the two-founder ones) through svc-identity; no `IDENTITY_URL` refuses `action_approval.identity_unwired`. Business maker-checker (`business.approve`/`reject`) is not this; it is `src/dual-control.ts`.
- **The ledger** (`src/ledger-client.ts`, `src/ledger-outcome.ts`, `src/ledger-door-fake.ts` for tests): svc-bank holds no balance; it reads balances and history from svc-ledger (`LEDGER_URL`) and posts only recipes from `packages/ledger-client/src/recipes/` (the most used: `bankTransfer`, `withdrawHold`/`Settle`/`Reverse`, `deposit`, the `loan*` and `earn*` recipes, `businessApprovalHold`/`Settle`, `payReceivedHold`). Every post has a bounded budget (10 s an attempt, 2 retries). The ledger's posting freeze is read through `ledgerPostingFreeze` (index.ts). Unconfirmed for Rust: svc-ledger's Rust `postRecipe` door takes only svc-academy and svc-agents today (`crates/svc-ledger/src/door/callers.rs`), and the TypeScript bank builds recipe entries locally and calls `post`; which door the Rust bank posts through is decided by the first money slice and must land at svc-ledger before the switch.
- **The partner** (`src/partner/partner-bank.ts`): at launch the person's money is held by a partner bank; the only partner today is the TEST stand-in (`src/partner/test-partner.ts`, its in-memory book `test-partner-book.ts`), on with `BANK_TEST_PARTNER=test` on dev and test stacks only. The custodial layer is kept and switched off (`LEDGER_CUSTODY_LAYER`, read in `src/env.ts`, `src/bank-root.ts`, `src/partner/partner-bank.ts`). The other stand-ins: pay-in (`src/ramps/payin-sim.ts`, `onramp-sim.ts`), payout (`src/cash-out/payout-sim.ts`), payroll (`src/salary/payroll-sim.ts`), direct debits (`src/direct-debits/scheme.ts`, `src/partner/test-partner-direct-debits.ts`), global sends (`src/personal-pay/global-sim.ts`), the card issuer simulator (`src/cards/issuer.ts`), the TEST pay-in chain watcher (`src/pay-yourself/test-pay-in.ts`). Each says TEST and refuses outside dev and test.
- **Owner values stay blank and refuse**: a blank fee, rate, limit, period, sanctions list or term refuses with its own code (`bank.sorter_terms_unset`, `bank.rule_terms_unset`, `bank.joint_unset`, `bank.destination_sanctions_unset`, `bank.personal_transfer_fee_unwired`, `rail.bank` for an unwired rail, ...). Batch sizes for jobs are owner values too (`src/job-batch-limit.ts`, `src/catalog-list-limit.ts`, `src/owner-list-limit.ts`). None is invented in Rust.
- **Calls out** (`src/index.ts`): svc-ledger (`LEDGER_URL`: posts, balances, history pages, holdings history, the asset registry, the posting freeze), svc-identity (`IDENTITY_URL`: recipient state, party handles, business directory, step-up and ops approvals, security lock, approval mail, trusted devices, extra controls, recovery claims, rank tier, affiliate accrue and payout), svc-pay (`PAY_URL`: deposit catalogue and addresses, withdrawal addresses), svc-trade (`TRADE_URL`: tickers and closes for loan marks, card conversion and portfolio, the convert port for auto-invest, realised P&L for pay-yourself), NATS (`NATS_URL`: `bankCardCashbackEarned`, `bankScheduledReminder`, `bankScheduledFailed`, and the personal request, personal transfer and margin call notices through `eventPersonalRequestNotices`, `eventPersonalTransferNotices`, `eventMarginCallSink`). Each is signed as svc-bank (`ledgerS2s`, `doorS2s`). svc-bank subscribes to no event (no consumer found in `src/`).
- **Callers**: the customer app and the admin console through svc-edge (`/api/bank`, `BANK_URL`, `services/svc-edge/src/routes.ts`) onto `/trpc`. Service-only calls: `business.approverEligibility` (svc-pay, `packages/contracts/src/bank-approver-eligibility-client.ts`), `ramps.destinations.sendable` (svc-pay, `bank-send-destination-client.ts`), `transfers.settleWithdrawal` (svc-pay, `bank-internal-withdrawal-client.ts`), `safety.transferSent` (svc-support, `bank-scam-transfer.ts`), `spaces.ownedAccounts` (svc-tax, `crates/svc-tax/src/clients/bank.rs`). svc-trade calls `convertCash.*` as the person (`services/svc-trade/src/convert/cash-leg-client.ts`). REST doors: svc-agents (`/internal/agents/team-membership`, `/internal/agents/usage-payment`), svc-identity (`/internal/closure-check`, `IDENTITY_CLOSURE_BANK_URL`), svc-ops (`/internal/restore/watermark`, `/internal/capital/partner-reserves`). Who calls the `/internal/jobs/*` routes in a deployed stack is not confirmed here (an outside scheduler, per the comment in index.ts above `run-due-transfers`).
- **Job doors** (`src/index.ts`): signed as a service (`requireService`, `src/internal-job-hmac.ts`), 401 before any flag check, then the job's own switch (503), the body (400 `bank.validation_failed`), and `BankError` as 400 (`sendBankJobRefuse`). `jobDoor(path, body, run)` is the shared shape.
- **Timers** (`src/index.ts`): the recovery jobs (`src/recovery-jobs.ts`, `startBankRecoveryJobs`, every `BANK_RECOVERY_JOBS_INTERVAL_MS` with `BANK_RECOVERY_JOBS_BATCH_LIMIT`, under a session advisory lock on its own one-connection pool; jobs `bank.recovery.resume_pending_loans`, `recover_loan_repayments`, `resume_pending_earn`, `announce_posted_transfers`, `redrive_cash_outs`, `sweep_received_holds`, `deliver_bank_api_webhooks`); the statement export worker (`src/statements/statement-jobs.ts`, `startStatementJobWorker`, `BANK_STATEMENT_JOBS_INTERVAL_MS`); the TEST pay-in chain poll (every second, only with the TEST pay-in rail).

## Where the port cannot be the TypeScript's

- tRPC batching is not served, as in every Rust door.
- The kit's matcher reads the `u` flag only as far as svc-bank's patterns need it: code points, `\p{Cc}` and `\P{Cc}`. A pattern with any other `u`-mode property escape refuses to load, so a schema that adds one fails `tests/input_parity.rs` rather than passing unread.
