**How to read the columns.**

- **Infra:** heads where it failed only for infra reasons. Cancelled jobs are counted separately at the bottom.

What "alone" leaves out. The seal and orchestration checks fail because others failed, so they don't count as another failure: `CI merge seal`, `CI jobs`, `Re-run the seal on this head`, `Bisect main`, `Post-merge checks` and `Review sign-off`. Every other failing check counts as a failure, including the CI review, the Test judge, Dependabot and the journeys family. That family is the `Journeys (*)` shards, `Real-app journeys`, `Build the stack once`, `Plan the shards`, `What this run runs` and `Journeys revert`. Their own infra reds also count, so "alone" is a lower bound. A step is alone only when it was the only failed step of its job, and its job the only failing check.
